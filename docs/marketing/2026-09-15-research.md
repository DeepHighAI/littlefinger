# SEO → AEO → GEO: evidence brief

Research date: 2026-09-15 (KST). Owner: Codex; independent review: adjacent Claude Code.
Read with [Claude's independent research](2026-09-15-claude-research.md).
This is a learning record before implementation, not evidence of marketing results.

## What changes our plan

- SEO supplies crawlable, useful public pages. AEO supplies clear answers to real user questions.
  GEO tests whether those pages are accurately cited in generated answers. These overlap; they
  are not three replacement technologies or three guaranteed traffic channels. [S1]
- Google now explicitly ignores `llms.txt` for ranking. No special schema or prescribed paragraph
  length is required. Prefer a few original product walkthroughs over generated keyword variants. [S1]
- Old advice is stale: FAQ rich results ended on 2026-05-07; a FAQ can still help readers. [S2]
- Google documents a generative AI inclusion setting and an impressions report, with worldwide
  rollout noted on 2026-08-31. Check the actual property; an absent report can mean insufficient
  impressions. Never interpret an absent report as zero traffic. [S3, S4]
- Bing citations are visibility observations, not installs, ranking positions, or endorsement. [S5]
- Search crawling and model-training crawling are separate controls. Being crawlable does not
  guarantee citation. Robots rules are not authorization or a privacy boundary. [S6, S7]

## Initial first-batch hypothesis — superseded by joint review

Fix discovery documents, add the home canonical at build time, keep token/account/error shells
out of search, and protect public legal-page accessibility. Do not put private URLs in sitemaps.
Prepare a small set of product-specific answer drafts and an aggregate weekly scorecard.
Hold content publishing until the research and review are recorded. Deploying the dirty tree
would also publish unrelated existing legal changes, so keep deployment a separately verified step.
The [joint decision](strategy.md#joint-decision-record--2026-09-15) replaces the initial shell-level
noindex idea with private-path response headers and selects HTTP canonical headers for the home.

We have not measured keyword demand, search positions, AI citations, installs, or activation.
All audience priorities and content opportunities remain hypotheses until console data and
real-user feedback support them. No outcome or deadline for indexing is promised.

Independent research caution: S12 is a survey interpretation, S13 an experimental preprint. They
support separating citation from economic outcomes, but neither supplies a growth forecast for
this product. We have not audited their complete datasets or reproduced their analyses.
