## Objective and operating assumptions

Turn a useful answer about making a mutual promise into a qualified Android creator, then a
promise accepted by the other person. Keep the product's existing north star: **weekly confirmed
promises (ACTIVE transitions)** (`01_상위기획서.md` §12). Installs alone do not prove value.
SEO → AEO → GEO is our order of investment, not an assertion that SEO is obsolete.

Start in Korean, on owned public pages, with no paid tools or media budget. These are reversible
planning assumptions, not PO-approved spending or evidence of market size. Public product pages
must use the ko/en catalogs; separate English indexable URLs can follow real demand.

Working draft positioning: **둘이 지키는 약속 기록**, matching the current web/spec. Claude observed
the live Korean Play listing leads with **보상을 걸고 지키는 약속**. The PO was asked which direction
to use; the first guide uses existing web/spec positioning under the subsequent publication
instruction. Cross-channel alignment remains a later decision.
Explain Android creation + installation-free web
participation, mutual approval, visible change history and neutral recording. Distinguish the
brand from similarly named businesses with consistent purpose, operator and Play package identity.
Do not promise login-free acceptance, automatic penalties, permanent free retention, guaranteed
relationship outcomes, a full iOS app, or a legal service.

## Measurement and weekly decisions

Use [measurement runbook](measurement.md) and its CSV templates. GSC has a first six-day observation
(Pacific time, 0 clicks / 2 impressions); other acquisition/activation baselines are not collected.
For the first 28 days, success means working measurement and useful indexed content, not a promised
number of visitors, citations or installs.

1. If public URLs cannot be crawled/indexed, repair technical or quality problems before adding pages.
2. If relevant impressions appear but few clicks follow, review intent/title/description; change
   one factor and compare equal windows, noting position/device/seasonality.
3. If store intent rises without acquisition, inspect device eligibility, listing and install flow.
4. If acquisitions rise without mutual activation, prioritize the invitation/approval experience.
   The spec's invite → approval target is at least 50%; this is an existing product target, not
   an observed result or a new marketing promise. Define the measured cohort before comparing it.
5. If AI visibility rises without qualified acquisition, keep it as visibility evidence only.
6. With sparse/suppressed data, report the limit and extend observation; do not invent statistical
   significance. Three repeated prompt answers are an observation, not market share.

## Joint decision record — 2026-09-15

Claude delivered a 40-entry independent ledger and reviewed Codex's 14-source brief and drafts.
Research Task `task_aa0576ed4b9d` settled successfully before the implementation task was sent.
Agreed: ordinary SEO foundations, truthful answer content, no fabricated citation lift, no new
trackers, no blanket noindex, Naver in measurement, and campaign attribution in the first batch.

Disposition of recommendations:

- Accept plain robots and a public-only sitemap. Start with the home URL only: sitemap completeness
  is not required. Extend it alongside verified public-page prerenders, not fictional draft URLs.
- Accept private-path `X-Robots-Tag` headers. Reject noindex on the common shell because it also
  serves public legal pages. Do not disallow paths that need a crawler to observe noindex.
- Use only HTTP `Link` canonical headers on `/` and `/index.html` for this batch. This avoids stale
  DOM metadata after SPA navigation without adding a head-management subsystem. Support by engines
  other than Google's web results remains unverified; broaden metadata in the next content batch.
- Add optional `utm_campaign` support and a home campaign; old source/medium-only calls remain
  compatible. Invite campaign tagging is a follow-up, not a reason to modify mobile flows now.
- Defer unique public-page titles, terms/deletion prerender, and guide publication to the next
  content batch, with ordinary localization/visual checks. They overlap pre-existing legal work.
- Add Naver AI surfaces, report lag and traffic-source columns to measurement. Search-engine share
  is not AI-assistant usage share; US click studies are not Korean demand forecasts.
- Keep the live-vs-repo positioning mismatch pending the PO's answer. Channel creation and training
  crawler preferences are optional future decisions, not blockers for this technical batch.
