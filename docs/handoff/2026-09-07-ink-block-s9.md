## Decisions made + why

- A05 keeps a content card, a people list and (non-ACTIVE) an approvals list on every status although several
  artboards omit them: the record body, the witness (`하린`) and the approval actions are what the tests and the
  record's purpose require. Routine, reported as 통보.
- CHECKING/BROKEN/UNRESOLVED/DECLINED/CANCELED render the per-status `HEADLINE` in a compact stamp (the gallery
  reuses the confirmed-record wording); DECLINED/CANCELED get a muted `remove` stamp the artboard lacks so the
  headline the tests assert still has a home.
- Fingerprint is shown on every stamp except PENDING (record integrity is the product); artboards omit it on compact.
- The finish request moved from the action bar into the no-end sky info card (gallery); the amend actions live in the
  협의 card; 숨기기/신고/차단 sit in a text/danger row under the main pair (no app-bar overflow menu — the tests press
  them directly and a menu would change the flow).
- Header date line is two Text nodes (label + value) so `'2026-09-01 (화)'` stays a single text for search and tests.
- SCR-I adds an app bar without a back control: the route is entered by deep link and may have no history.
- MOD-01 uses the segmented control with `tab` roles (P6-A precedent on A02); MOD-02 keeps the '초대 링크 공유하기'
  label (C-4: OS share sheet) on the Kakao-styled CTA; the unlock button stays visible below capacity as before.
- Proof thumbs and the claim evidence box keep the 3px tile shadow inside a muted 2px box — unverified on device.
